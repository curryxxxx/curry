System overview

CURRY

MetaTrader 5 Forex automation bot
Paper trading is the default

A configurable trading workflow that reads closed market bars, evaluates one selected strategy, applies entry and account-risk checks, then simulates or submits an order.

Important: The included strategy rules are examples, not claims of profitability. Trading involves substantial risk. Backtest and validate with realistic spread, slippage, commissions, broker constraints, and out-of-sample data.

Main orchestration is in main.py; supporting modules provide configuration, indicators, market data, strategy evaluation, risk checks, execution, filters, logging, and backtesting.

Runtime sequence

  1. Load and validate settings; connect to the MT5 terminal.
  2. Refresh the economic calendar and determine active trading sessions.
  3. Update paper positions, account status, and daily P&L.
  4. For each configured symbol, check session/news rules and fetch closed OHLC bars.
  5. Calculate indicators; evaluate the configured strategy and produce a signal if its conditions match.
  6. Reject duplicate-bar signals and apply risk checks; calculate volume from account risk and MT5 contract data.
  7. Open a paper position by default, or submit a live market order with broker-side stop-loss and take-profit.

Signal and risk model

Signal construction

Reference price is the latest closed-bar close. The default ATR stop multiplier is 1.5 and target reward/risk is 2.0.

BUY SL = price − ATR × multiplier; TP = price + stop distance × reward/risk.
SELL SL = price + ATR × multiplier; TP = price − stop distance × reward/risk.

Entry protections

Weekend closure, maximum spread, open-position cap, daily loss cap, session allow-list, and high-impact news blackout.

Default risk per trade is 0.5% of account equity (configurable from 0.5% to 1%). Lot sizing uses MT5's profit calculator and broker volume limits.

Operating modes and safety

Paper mode (default)

  • Simulates positions; does not place MT5 orders.
  • Still uses the connected MT5 terminal for quotes, account info, and sizing.
  • Paper exits and trade history are tracked locally.

Live mode (explicit opt-in)

  • Requires CURRY_TRADING_MODE=live and CURRY_LIVE_TRADING_CONFIRM=I_ACCEPT_LIVE_RISK.
  • Live orders include broker-side SL/TP; failures are reported as errors.
  • Verify account, symbols, and broker constraints; test paper mode first.

Backtesting and limits

The sequential OHLC backtester enters on the next bar and uses conservative stop-first handling when both exit levels are reached within a bar. Spread is an explicit input; commissions and all execution effects may not be fully modeled. Historical results do not guarantee future performance.

Typical start: Install Python 3.12+, install requirements, open and sign in to MetaTrader 5, then run python main.py. Use environment settings to choose a strategy, timeframe, and symbols. Never store broker credentials in source code.
